About

A physician drawn to the full arc of care

I am a physician with deep roots in pediatric critical care, now pursuing fellowship training in Hospice & Palliative Medicine at Atrium Health in Charlotte, NC. My work across the spectrum of acuity — from ICU to end-of-life — reflects a sustained interest in what it means to care well for patients and families at the moments that matter most.

I spent several years as an attending pediatric intensivist at NYU Langone Health, caring for critically ill children and their families in one of the country's preeminent academic medical centers. That work sharpened my clinical instincts and deepened my conviction that excellent medicine is inseparable from honest, human-centered communication.

In July 2026, I began a fellowship in Hospice & Palliative Care, following a pull toward the questions that don't resolve in the ICU: how we accompany people through serious illness, how we hold space for uncertainty, and how we help patients and families make decisions that align with what they value most.

Publications

Research & Writing

Critical Care Medicine 2024

Palliative Care Integration in the Pediatric Intensive Care Unit: Patterns, Barriers, and Outcomes

A retrospective cohort analysis examining rates and determinants of palliative care consultation in a quaternary PICU, with attention to family-reported experience and end-of-life care quality metrics.

Pediatrics 2022

Goals-of-Care Conversations in Pediatric Critical Care: A Qualitative Study of Family Perspectives

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with families of PICU patients reveals domains of communication that families identify as most supportive during high-stakes clinical decision-making.

JAMA Pediatrics 2021

SARS-CoV-2 in Critically Ill Pediatric Patients: Clinical Course and Outcomes

Multicenter case series describing clinical presentation, ventilator management, and outcomes for pediatric patients admitted to the PICU with COVID-19 and MIS-C during the first two years of the pandemic.
